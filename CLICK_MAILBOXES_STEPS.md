# Exact Steps: Click "Mailboxes" in Exchange Admin Center

## Step 1: You're in the Right Place
✅ You clicked "Recipients"  
✅ You see "Mailboxes, Groups, Resources, and Contacts"  
✅ Now click **"Mailboxes"**

## Step 2: Find Your User
1. You'll see a list of mailboxes
2. Look for: **info@projectplanner.us**
3. OR search in the search box at the top: **`info`**
4. Click on the mailbox when you find it

## Step 3: Enable SMTP Auth
After clicking the mailbox, you'll see details on the right side:

1. Look for tabs like: [General] [Mail] [Groups] [Membership] [...]
2. Click on the **"Mail"** tab (NOT "General")
3. Scroll down to find **"Email connectivity"** or **"Client access"** section
4. Look for **"SMTP AUTH"** or **"Authenticated SMTP"**
5. **Enable it** (check the box ☑️ or toggle ON)
6. Click **"Save"** at the top

---

## If You Don't See "Mailboxes" Tab
You might be in the **new Exchange Admin Center**. Try this:

### Alternative Path:
1. Click **"Recipients"** (left sidebar)
2. The page should show tabs at the top: [Mailboxes] [Groups] [Resources] [Contacts]
3. Make sure you're on the **"Mailboxes"** tab (should be selected by default)
4. You should see your mailboxes listed
5. Click on **info@projectplanner.us**
6. Then follow Step 3 above
