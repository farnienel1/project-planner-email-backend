# Where to Find SMTP Settings When "Mail" Tab Doesn't Show

## You're Here: Recipients → Mailboxes → [Your Mailbox Selected]
✅ Clicked "Recipients"
✅ Clicked "Mailboxes" 
✅ Found and selected your mailbox

## Next Steps - What to Look For

Since there's no "Mail" tab, look for these alternatives:

### Option 1: Look for "Mailbox Features" Tab
- Click on tabs at the bottom or top of the details panel
- Look for **"Mailbox Features"** or **"Features"**
- Inside there should be SMTP settings

### Option 2: Look at the Right Panel Details
When you click your mailbox, look at the **details panel on the right side**. You should see:
- User info at the top
- Sections below like:
  - **"Email connectivity"**
  - **"Client access"**
  - **"Mail features"**
  - **"Manage mailbox features"**

Click on any of these to find SMTP settings.

### Option 3: Click "Manage mailbox features"
- Look for a button or link that says **"Manage mailbox features"**
- Or **"Manage"** or **"Edit"** near the top
- Click it to see a popup with more options

### Option 4: Look at the Top Buttons
- Look for buttons at the top of the details panel like:
  - **"Manage"**
  - **"Edit"**
  - **"Manage properties"**
  - **"Configure"**
- Click any of these

### Option 5: Scroll Down
- The SMTP settings might be lower on the page
- Scroll down to see if there's an "Email connectivity" or "Client access" section

### Option 6: Search for "SMTP" or "Authenticated"
- Use the browser's search (Cmd+F on Mac)
- Search for **"SMTP"** or **"Auth"** or **"Authenticated"**
- It will highlight where the setting is

---

## Alternative: Enable SMTP Auth Organization-Wide

If you can't find it for your specific user, enable it for everyone:

### Step 1: Go to Settings
In Exchange Admin Center:
1. Look at the left sidebar
2. Find **"Settings"** or **"Organization"** or **"Mail flow"**
3. Click on it

### Step 2: Find SMTP Auth Settings
Look for:
- **"SMTP authentication"**
- **"Authenticated SMTP"**  
- **"Client authentication settings"**
- **"Mail flow settings"**

### Step 3: Enable It
Enable SMTP authentication for authenticated users or all users.

---

## What the SMTP Setting Looks Like

When you find it, you'll see something like:

```
☑ Enable authenticated client SMTP submission (SMTP AUTH)
  When enabled, users can use email clients that don't use 
  modern authentication to send email from this mailbox.
```

Or a toggle/switch to turn ON.
